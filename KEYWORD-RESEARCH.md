# Kriyvo AI — Keyword research (Oct 2026)

Method: live Google results for each search, checked for *who ranks* and *what kind of page wins*.
No search-volume tool was used — add monthly volumes from Google Keyword Planner (free) to prioritise.
Competition: **Low** = marketplaces / thin pages rank, a focused page can win · **Med** = some real
agency pages · **High** = big brands and platforms.

## 1. Local India — who ranks today

| Search | What ranks now | Competition | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| freelance PHP developer Noida | Truelancer, Hubstaff Talent profiles, job boards | **Low** | No real freelancer site ranks — a dedicated page can |
| freelance WordPress developer Delhi NCR | Truelancer, IndiaMART, Arc.dev, IRPR agency city pages | **Low–Med** | Agency city pages are thin and template-like |
| freelance website developer Gurgaon | Freelancer.com, Truelancer, Craigslist, IndiaMART | **Low** | Same pattern — marketplaces only |
| website developer East Delhi / Laxmi Nagar | Mayank Digital Labs (dedicated East Delhi page), IRPR, IndiaMART | **Low** | **Strongest fit** — New Ashok Nagar *is* East Delhi |
| AI chatbot / WhatsApp chatbot company Delhi | Zartek, JoomDev, AiSensy, Fiverr gigs | **Med** | Few Delhi-specific pages; your live demo is a differentiator |
| n8n automation expert India | Fiverr, n8n community job posts, Upwork | **Low–Med** | Almost no portfolio sites — strong niche |

**Pattern:** for every local search, marketplace profiles fill the first page. Two consequences:
1. A genuine page on your own site, with real projects and a real address, can outrank thin profiles.
2. Listing yourself **on those same marketplaces** (Truelancer, IndiaMART, Freelancer.in) also puts
   you on page one quickly — add them to the off-page checklist.
