## 2. His weaknesses

- **The city pages are near-duplicates.** The Noida, Gurgaon and Delhi pages have the same structure, the same pricing, the same reviews and the same process. Only the city name and a few reworded sentences change. That's the doorway-page pattern Google demotes, so it can stop working in any update.
- **Thin, generic, typo-filled copy** ("websit developer", "wesbsite", "wdp-timeline"), and no real explanation of how anything is built.
- **No blog.** `/blogs/` is empty ("It seems we can't find what you're looking for").
- **Services page is 242 words** listing SEO, Adwords, SMM, logo design and Shopify with no detail.
- **No case studies.** Just client names and screenshots.
- **Reviews are 2–3 years old**, and the same 9 reviews appear on every city page.
- **No AI, chatbot, WhatsApp bot or automation offer.**
