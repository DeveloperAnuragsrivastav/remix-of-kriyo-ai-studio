# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Two buyers, weighted equally:
- **Local small businesses in Delhi NCR** (shops, clinics, restaurants, coaching centres, service firms) who want a website, WhatsApp bot or chatbot quickly and cheaply, mostly arriving from Google local searches and WhatsApp.
- **Growing companies in India and abroad** who need AI chatbots, voice agents, LLM agents and n8n / Python automation built properly, and judge on proof of engineering.

Both decide by comparing price, proof and how fast they can talk to the builder.

## Product Purpose
Kriyvo AI is an independent studio in New Ashok Nagar, East Delhi: one web developer and AI automation engineer who designs, builds and hands over websites, WordPress/PHP sites, AI chatbots, WhatsApp bots, voice agents and n8n workflow automation. The site's job is to turn a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation or an estimate request.

## Positioning
One builder covers both the website and the AI/automation behind it, at published fixed prices, delivered in days, with a documented handover. Local web-only freelancers (e.g. niketankmogha.com) don't offer AI or automation; AI agencies don't publish ₹999 websites.

## Operating Context
Visitors land from Google (local and service searches), Product Hunt, Instagram and WhatsApp shares, mostly on phones. All sales happen on WhatsApp (+91 86040 50596) or email; there is no checkout. Delivery is fully remote — no in-person meetings.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stack: TanStack Start (React), Tailwind v4, deployed on Netlify. Pages: home, services (6), pricing, portfolio (6), case studies (4), blog (25), about, estimate, contact, two location pages.
- Published prices: websites from ₹999/page, business site ₹4,999, e-commerce ₹9,999; website AI chatbot ₹1,999, WhatsApp AI chatbot ₹4,999, AI business automation ₹3,999, custom AI automation ₹9,999.
- Interactive pieces that must keep working: project estimator (sends brief to WhatsApp), website chatbot / outreach playground demo, contact form → WhatsApp.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Kriyvo AI. The orange "K" mark (`src/assets/kriyvo-mark.webp`) stays.
- Orange stays the brand colour.
- Voice: plain, direct, first person ("I build…"), no hype.
- Visual bar set by the owner (Oct 2026): premium AI-product feel at the level of sarvam.ai and elevenlabs.io, with strong animation; must not look like a generic AI-generated site.

## Evidence on Hand
- 115+ projects delivered, 30+ clients (stated on site).
- Google reviews: 5.0 from 5 reviews (copied verbatim in `src/lib/kriyo-data.ts`).
- Portfolio: KOCA restaurant (Yuvraj Singh venue), Gignaati WhatsApp funnel, AI phone agent (NDA), Aurum Conventions outreach, BMRC hospital feedback pipeline, website chatbot — with images in `src/assets/portfolio/`.
- Product Hunt listing (badge in footer and hero).
- No client logos, video, or press. Do not invent testimonials, metrics or clients.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work, not adjectives: real projects, real prices, real reviews.
2. Every page ends one tap from WhatsApp.
3. Speak to the ₹999 buyer and the automation buyer without making either feel out of place.
4. Honest scope: say what's included and what costs extra.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Phone-first, works on slow Indian mobile connections; readable for non-native English speakers.
