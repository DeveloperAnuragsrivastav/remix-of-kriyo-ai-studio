# Homepage — surface brief

Scope: `/` homepage, Persuade mode. All inner pages share the same world via AgencyLayout, Kit, PageIntro, ProjectCTA, PricingPlans, Testimonials, LocalPage.
Audience: local Delhi NCR businesses (₹999 websites, WhatsApp bots) and growing companies buying AI/automation, weighted equally.
Action: WhatsApp conversation or estimate. Proof: live demos of real projects, published prices, Google reviews.
User pin (overrides the roll): premium AI-product feel at the level of sarvam.ai (placement, fonts, colour, button style, hover) and elevenlabs.io; must not look generic or Claude-made. K mark and orange stay. Owner rejected an abstract AI orb except as the voice of the voice-agent demo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kriyvo presented with the calm polish of an AI product company: every service is shown working on its real screen (a laptop running the site, chat, WhatsApp Web, an n8n canvas, a call console) and can be tried live, instead of the icon-card grid, glow blob and logo ticker the category ships.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral near-white ground, ink near-black, warm stone panels; orange only as signal (CTA tag dot, live states, the voice orb, "Popular"), saffron for highlights on ink. Source Serif 4 at regular weight for display, Host Grotesk for body. Dark gradient pill and white ringed pill buttons with a cursor-following light. 20–28px rounded panels, ruled lists, one dark band per page at most, no eyebrows above headings, no icon tiles.

STORY: Visitor reads what this is and the starting price, watches or picks a service on the laptop stage, sees services as an index, real work in a bento they can try live, posted prices, the process, Google reviews, then plans a project or messages on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim offer bar; centred hero with a small location tag, two-line serif headline, one sentence with prices, dark "Chat on WhatsApp" + light "See prices", a quiet rating/projects line; below, a stone stage with a service list on the left and a large laptop on the right.

FORM: user-pinned Sarvam/ElevenLabs register; overrides roll item 7; seed key 8b4b12d2. Signature interactions: the laptop demo stage (auto-advancing scripted samples per service) and "Try it now" live demos (AI assistant, WhatsApp funnel, feedback classifier, ElevenLabs voice agent, LinkedIn outreach).
