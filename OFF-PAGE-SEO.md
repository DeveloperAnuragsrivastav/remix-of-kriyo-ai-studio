# Kriyvo AI — Off-page SEO & backlink checklist

Every listing must use the **same details**, character for character. Google matches these to
trust that it's one real business.

```
Name:      Kriyvo AI
Website:   https://kriyvoai.online
Phone:     +91 86040 50596
Email:     kriyvo.ai@gmail.com
Address:   New Ashok Nagar, New Delhi 110096, India
Category:  Website designer / Software company
Short bio: AI automation & web development studio in New Delhi. Custom websites, WordPress,
           AI chatbots, WhatsApp bots & n8n workflows for service businesses. From ₹15K.
```

Do **not** buy links, use link farms, PBNs, or "1000 backlinks for ₹500" offers — Google penalises
them and the damage is hard to undo. Every link below is free and comes from a real profile or
real content.

## Ongoing — links you earn (best long-term)

1. **Client credit links.** Ask KOCA, Aurum Conventions and Gignaati for a small footer line
   "Website / automation by Kriyvo AI" linking to `https://kriyvoai.online`. Real client links are
   the most valuable backlinks you can get.
2. **Republish your 22 articles** on DEV, Hashnode and Medium **with the canonical URL set to
   kriyvoai.online** (so your site gets the credit). One article a week.
3. **n8n community.** Share 2–3 of your workflows as templates on n8n.io/workflows and in the
   community forum, linking to the related article.
4. **Answer real questions** on Reddit (r/n8n, r/automation, r/webdev), Quora and Stack Overflow.
   Answer fully first; link only when the article genuinely helps.
5. **Guest articles** for Indian startup/tech blogs (YourStory community, Inc42 contributor,
   Analytics India Magazine) — one solid piece per month.
6. **Podcasts / meetups** — Delhi AI & no-code meetups list speakers on their event pages.

## Reviews (Google profile)

Send the "Get more reviews" link from your Google profile to each real client. Ask them to
mention the service ("our WhatsApp bot", "our website") — it helps local ranking. Reply to every
review within a few days.

## What to track (monthly)

- Search Console → Performance: clicks, impressions, top queries.
- Search Console → Links: new linking sites.
- Google profile → Performance: calls, website clicks, direction requests.
- Count of live listings from this file (aim: 15+ in the first month).
